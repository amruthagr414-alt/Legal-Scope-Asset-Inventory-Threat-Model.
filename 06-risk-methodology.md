# 6. Risk Rating Methodology

## 6.1 Rating scale

Likelihood and impact are rated from 1 to 3.

| Score | Likelihood | Impact |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Low — unlikely in the defined lab context | Low — limited effect |
| 2 | Medium — plausible | Medium — noticeable effect on lab data or availability |
| 3 | High — more likely without controls | High — significant confidentiality, integrity, or availability effect |

**Risk score = Likelihood × Impact**

| Score | Rating |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Low |
| 3–4 | Medium |
| 6–9 | High |

## 6.2 Interpretation

These scores are qualitative planning estimates used to prioritize mitigations. They do not represent measured exploitability, a penetration-test result, or a formal risk acceptance decision.

## 6.3 Review

Reassess each threat after controls are implemented. Record the evidence supporting any change in likelihood or impact, and obtain owner or supervisor approval for residual-risk acceptance.
